FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before joining.

Is Flexori free to use?+
Yes — Flexori is completely free. Build your Remote Digital Passport, get your Employability Score, access the Intelligence Center, and follow the Onboarding Path. No credit card. No limits. No catch. Ever.
What languages does Flexori support?+
Flexori launches with full support for English, Spanish, French, and Portuguese. More languages will follow.
What is the Remote Employability Score?+
Flexori's proprietary index measuring your readiness for remote work across 8 dimensions: communication, reliability, remote tool proficiency, portfolio, references, timezone flexibility, language skills, and remote history.
I'm new to remote work. Is Flexori for me?+
Absolutely. The Remote Onboarding Path guides you step by step — from workspace setup, to building your digital passport, to landing your first remote role.
Can my company post jobs on Flexori?+
Yes. Post remote jobs, set a minimum Employability Score, filter by timezone and availability, and review verified Digital Passports instead of traditional CVs.
Is Flexori available now?+
Yes — Flexori is live and free to use right now. Join today and get founding member status with priority access to every new feature we ship.
